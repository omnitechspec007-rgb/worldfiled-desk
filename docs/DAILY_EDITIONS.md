# Daily editions: video bulletin and text briefing

## Product decision
One daily research cycle feeds two independently curated editions. Each edition has its own story selection, order, editorial approval and immutable prompt history. The editions may share stories, but neither is generated by copying the other's selection.

- **Video bulletin:** target 6-8 developments; approximately 8-12 minutes. Lead stories receive deeper treatment; supporting stories are concise. Never pad a quiet news day.
- **Text briefing:** target 12-18 developments; approximately 5-8 minutes to read. Lead stories receive context; standard developments receive concise briefs; minor but useful updates receive short briefs. Never add weak stories to hit a quota.
- **Shared pool:** both editions draw from the same deduplicated, evidence-linked research pool. Each selected development retains its source list and evidence limitations.
- **Breaking news:** a major verified development can trigger a clearly labelled standalone update; it does not force a second full daily edition.
- **Human gate:** both editions require separate human approval before prompt generation. No automatic publication.

## Editorial safeguards
- Distinguish a development from the number of publishers reporting it. Duplicate coverage is not multiple stories or independent corroboration.
- Evidence strength and recency remain visible during selection.
- Editors can select, exclude and reorder stories. The app must not silently remove weak stories.
- Video capacity is a target, not a mandatory quota. Text capacity is a ceiling, not a target.
- Existing saved editions and prompt history remain immutable historical records.

## Implementation
- `edition_kind` distinguishes `video` and `text` editions and is stamped into each generated prompt record.
- Video prompts use the existing creative blueprint and presenter workflow.
- Text prompts use a separate editorial brief: headline, standfirst, lead stories, concise briefs, source attribution, claim-to-source table, uncertainty notes and fact-check checklist. No voice, scene, music or video instructions are included.
- Migration 006 is additive and must be run after 004 and 005. It preserves existing editions as `video`.
