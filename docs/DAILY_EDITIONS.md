# Daily editions: static news roundup and written briefing

## Product decision
One daily research cycle feeds two independently curated products. Each edition has its own story selection, order, editorial approval and immutable prompt history. The products may share stories, but neither is generated by copying the other's selection.

- **Six-second video roundup:** one vertical 9:16 static editorial composition, normally 8-10 concise international developments. It appears immediately, remains completely still for six seconds, and ends. No presenter, narration, moving camera, animated text, slideshow, scene changes or transitions. Optional sound is limited to a quiet editorial sound signature or restrained effects; no music bed.
- **Written briefing:** target 12-18 developments, approximately 5-8 minutes to read. Lead developments receive context; standard developments receive concise briefs; minor but useful updates receive short briefs. Never pad a quiet news day.
- **Shared pool:** both products draw from the same deduplicated, evidence-linked research pool. Each selected development retains its source list and evidence limitations.
- **Breaking news:** a major verified development can trigger a clearly labelled standalone update; it does not force a second full daily edition.
- **Human gate:** both editions require separate human approval. No automatic publication.

## Video composition
- One 1080 × 1920 (9:16) composition, not a sequence of scenes.
- Clear edition headline and date; compact individual story cards; restrained background photograph or editorial texture; small brand signature.
- Brand palette: Midnight Navy #0B1F38, Archive Cream #F7F4ED, Archival Gold #FFC857, Signal Teal #4CC9B0.
- Readability takes priority over story count. Each card should communicate a concise factual headline and one supporting line; location and source attribution appear where available.
- Editor can revise the headline and supporting text before export. Do not alter evidence or invent details to make text fit.
- The visual remains unchanged from first frame to last. Export target is exactly six seconds. Optional sound effects are quiet and non-intrusive.
- Current browser export is WebM; MP4 conversion is not part of this browser-only implementation. A PNG poster can also be downloaded.
- Preview the actual rendered frame before export. Use local image uploads or CORS-enabled image URLs for backgrounds.

## Editorial safeguards
- Distinguish a development from the number of publishers reporting it. Duplicate coverage is not multiple stories or independent corroboration.
- Evidence strength and recency remain visible during selection.
- Editors can select, exclude and reorder stories. The app must not silently remove weak stories.
- Ten stories is a practical upper target, not a mandatory quota. A quiet news day should produce a shorter roundup.
- Existing saved editions and prompt history remain immutable historical records.

## Implementation
- `edition_kind` distinguishes `video` and `text` editions and is stamped into each generated prompt record.
- Video profile opens the static roundup composer after approval; it uses the selected developments as editable cards and exports one still frame for six seconds.
- Text prompts use a separate editorial brief: headline, standfirst, lead stories, concise briefs, source attribution, claim-to-source table, uncertainty notes and fact-check checklist. No voice, scene, music or video instructions are included.
- Existing long-form video prompt paths are retained as legacy options for saved work and backward compatibility; they are not the default text-led video product.
