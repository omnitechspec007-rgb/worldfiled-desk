# Format Bible v1 (draft): animated map explainer

Derived from 4 reference videos (59 to 93 seconds, 9:16, supplied as 360x640 copies). The machine-readable version the
Prompt Builder uses is `netlify/lib/format-bible.mts`. Every entry there is tagged MEASURED, OBSERVED or INFERRED.

## What was measured from the files
- Length 59 to 93 seconds, 30 fps. No hard cuts at all (a scene-cut detector found none): the camera moves continuously.
- Captions change about 2 times per second (median gap 0.4 s).
- Audio is continuous (only 2 to 3 percent quiet), speech-like in rhythm, at a steady level of about -16.4 dBFS in all four.
- Colours: navy ocean about #0B161F, panels #131F2A, relief grey #4B4842, text #E1E8EC, gold about #CF9C23.

## What was read by eye
Top row with coordinates, scale bar and a tiny source line, plus an outlined gold series badge. One centred caption line
(bold condensed caps, key words gold). A dark shaded-relief map with the story's country filled gold. Gold-outlined cards
with a tiny label and big white text. A marker with a city label. A gold progress line along the top. A small gold logo
mark. Opens on a globe, ends on a recap grid and a vote or "your take" question.

## Beat structure
hook, who said what, the reason, the pushback, what to do, reassurance, recap, your take (see the bible for timings).

## Not known (honest list)
- The spoken words: speech could not be transcribed here. Share the script for any video to calibrate narration style.
- Whether there is background music, and which voice or tool made the voice-over.
- Exact fonts, animation easing and card timing: estimated from 360-pixel video.
- The reference videos carry a series name and logo mark. Those are not copied: the builder uses the house brand.

## Ideas not yet used
Audience performance data (views, shares, saves per video) should be stored separately and used to tune which beats and
topics work. It must never be mixed into verification.
